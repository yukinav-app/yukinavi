Aomori Spring Ski Resort

Aomori ・ About 40 min by car/taxi from JR Hirosaki Station or 20 min from JR Ajigasawa Station; a reservation-only tour shuttle from Shin-Aomori Station and Aomori Airport runs in 2026-27

A large resort on the northwestern slopes of Mt. Iwaki with a gondola to 921 m, known for deep snow and tree skiing, with the Rockwood Hotel & Spa next to the slopes.
