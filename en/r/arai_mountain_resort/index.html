Arai Mountain Resort

Niigata ・ About 8 min by car from Arai IC (Joshin-etsu Expressway); about 30 min by shuttle bus from Joetsu-Myoko Station (Hokuriku Shinkansen)

A powder-focused resort on snowy Mt. Okenashi with extensive ungroomed terrain, slopes up to 46 degrees and runs up to 7 km, plus a hotel, hot springs and a pool.
