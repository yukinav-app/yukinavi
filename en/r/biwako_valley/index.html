Biwako Valley

Shiga ・ About 5 min from Shiga IC on the Kosei Road (via Meishin Kyoto-Higashi IC) to the ropeway base station; about 10 min by local bus from JR Shiga Station

A lake-view ski area reached by ropeway, overlooking Lake Biwa and close to Kyoto/Osaka. Courses include the 1,400 m Champion course and the mogul-friendly Horai North slope, plus a snow play area.
