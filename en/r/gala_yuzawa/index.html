GALA Yuzawa Snow Resort

Niigata ・ About 3 km / 5 min from Kan-Etsu Expwy Yuzawa IC / About 10 min by free shuttle bus from JR Echigo-Yuzawa Sta.

The defining feature is direct access from the shinkansen platform — walk out of the ticket gate and you're at the ski center. Rentals and a day-use hot spring are in the same station-connected building.
