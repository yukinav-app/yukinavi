Hakkoda Ski Area (Hakkoda Ropeway & Hakkoda International)

Aomori ・ About 40 min by car from central Aomori and under 1 hour from Aomori Airport; JR Bus (for Lake Towada) to Hakkoda Ropeway-eki-mae

Ad: this page contains affiliate links.

A mountain-skiing classic: the Hakkoda Ropeway (summit station 1,324 m) accesses long ungroomed runs through snow monsters and powder, while the adjacent Hakkoda International area offers groomed runs from late November to Golden Week.
