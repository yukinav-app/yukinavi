IOX-AROSA

Toyama ・ About 42.9 km (approx. 50 min) from Toyama IC via the Hokuriku and Tokai-Hokuriku expressways and Fukumitsu Smart IC

A Nanto resort where a gondola takes you straight to the top, with mainly beginner and intermediate runs and night skiing. One-day tickets are 5,500 yen at New Year and on January-February weekends and holidays, 5,000 yen otherwise.
