Itoigawa Seaside Valley Ski Area

Niigata ・ About 15-20 min by car from Itoigawa IC (Hokuriku Expressway); about 35 min by bus from Itoigawa Station (Hokuriku Shinkansen)

A ski area with 360-degree views of the Sea of Japan and mountains from the top. Mostly beginner-intermediate, it also has a roughly 3 km long run and an ungroomed steep, with the Shio-no-Michi Onsen White Cliff hotel beside the slopes.
