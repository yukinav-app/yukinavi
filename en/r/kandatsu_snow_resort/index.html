Kandatsu Snow Resort

Niigata ・ About 1 km (3 min) from Yuzawa IC on the Kan-Etsu Expressway; about 7 min by free shuttle bus from JR Echigo-Yuzawa Station

A 100% natural-snow resort about 3 minutes from Yuzawa IC, one of the closest to the Kanto region. It has 16 courses, terrain parks and an on-site bath and sauna, Kami-no-Yu.
