Kutsuki Ski Area

Shiga ・ From the Aoyagi-kita intersection on Route 161 (Adogawa) toward Kutsuki; Prefectural Road 295 is closed in winter. Tire chains required

A small, family- and beginner-friendly ski area with slopes right next to the parking lot. It has a sled-only kids area, and the top of the lift offers views of Lake Biwa and Chikubu Island.
