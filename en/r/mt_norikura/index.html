Mt. Norikura Snow Resort

Nagano ・ About 60 minutes from the Matsumoto IC (Nagano Expwy).

A ski area on the Norikura plateau in Matsumoto, locally run since 2025, where lift tickets are free for anyone 18 or under; in spring it is also a gateway to Mt. Norikura backcountry.
