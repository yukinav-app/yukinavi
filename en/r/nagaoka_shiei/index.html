Nagaoka Municipal Ski Area

Niigata ・ About 30 min from Nagaoka IC (Kan-Etsu Expressway); about 20 min by taxi from the east exit of JR Nagaoka Station

A municipal ski area about 15 minutes by car from central Nagaoka. Compact, with two pair lifts and four slopes, it suits beginners and families with small children.
