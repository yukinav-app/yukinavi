Okukannabe Ski Area

Hyogo ・ About 13 km from Hidaka-Kannabe-Kogen IC on the Kitakinki-Toyooka Expressway

The highest ski area on the Kannabe plateau, with the 1,200 m Tochinoki run and the 35-degree Dangan course; powder can linger after snowfall. Day passes cost the same on weekdays and weekends; no night skiing.
