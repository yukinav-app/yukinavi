Rokkosan Snow Park

Hyogo ・ About 35 min by car from Kobe, 60 min from central Osaka / Rokko Sanjo bus from Rokko Cable Sanjo Sta. to Snow Park-mae stop, 1 min walk

One of the closest ski areas to Kobe and Osaka. Compact, with just two gentle courses; pricing combines a park entry fee plus lift ticket (about ¥4,400 on weekdays). A separate 'Snowland' area is set aside for sledding.
