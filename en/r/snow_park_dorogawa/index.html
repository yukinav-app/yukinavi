Snow Park Dorogawa

Nara ・ About 80 min (approx. 42 km) from Keinawa Expressway Gose-Minami IC via Route 309 and Prefectural Road 21

One of the few ski areas in Nara Prefecture, run by the Dorogawa property ward of Tenkawa Village. A small, roughly 400 m slope suited to beginners and families. A separate entry fee (1,200 yen for adults) applies, and operation depends on snowfall.
