Tokusagamine Ski Area

Yamaguchi ・ No bus service; taxi available near JR Tokusa Station

The westernmost ski area on Honshu, running on 100% natural snow and open only when snowfall allows; one 470 m pair lift, and grassy beginner runs skiable with just 20–30 cm of snow.
