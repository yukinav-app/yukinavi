Up Kannabe Ski Area

Hyogo ・ About 11 km / 15 min from Hidaka-Kannabe-Kogen IC on the Kitakinki-Toyooka Expressway

A family- and beginner-oriented area where about 80% of the wide, gentle slopes are beginner-intermediate. It has moving walkways and a 'snow amusement park', with night skiing on Fridays and Saturdays.
