Yachiho Kogen Ski Area

Nagano ・ About 17 minutes from the Yachiho-Kogen IC (Chubu-Odan Expwy).

A ski area at 1,630-1,830 m on the northern side of the Yatsugatake range that opens early, at the end of November. Features a 1.3 km cruising run, a dedicated mogul course and the '8 Park' terrain park.
